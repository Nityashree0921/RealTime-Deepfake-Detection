2. ANALYSIS OF V5 VS V6 PERFORMANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Baseline V5 Issues:
- V5 predictions hovered tightly between 0.44 and 0.56 (mean ~0.50) on all images.
- It suffered from severe label confusion in its testing scripts (treating label 0 as REAL in some scripts and FAKE in others).
- In the real-time webcam detector, the confidence was inverted, causing random flickering between REAL and FAKE on normal webcam faces.

B. V6 Pipeline Advancements:
- Clean, deterministic video-level dataset split (0 video ID leakage).
- Fixed the mathematical label convention across the entire codebase:
  - Class 0 = FAKE
  - Class 1 = REAL
  - Model Sigmoid Output = P(REAL)
  - P(FAKE) = 1.0 - P(REAL)
- Integrated internal `Rescaling(1/127.5, offset=-1)` without duplicate external normalization.
- Solved false-alarm deepfake alerts on live webcam faces: V6 achieved a 100% REAL detection rate (0% false fake rate across 200 real webcam frames).
- Integrated temporal smoothing (10-frame moving average) in the real-time detector to eliminate flickering.


3. SCIENTIFIC HONESTY & BOTTLENECK IDENTIFICATION (COLLEGE MAJOR PROJECT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
While V6 resolves all architectural, preprocessing, label interpretation, and webcam false-alarm issues, the overall ROC-AUC on the unseen video test set remains near chance (~0.45 - 0.55).

ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS OF THE BOTTLENECK:
1. Dataset Sample Size (448 Face Frames across only 66 video subsets):
   - 448 frames is extremely small for deep neural network generalization on complex video compression and blending artifacts.
   - Deep neural networks easily overfit to background lighting, facial identity, skin tone, or camera artifacts present in the few training videos rather than learning generalizable deepfake forgery artifacts.
2. Compression & Domain Shift:
   - Deepfake artifacts in datasets like FaceForensics++, Celeb-DF, or DFDC vary heavily depending on the synthesis tool (DeepFaceLab, Faceswap, GAN blending) and compression levels (C23, C40).
3. Video Diversity:
   - Having only ~32 unique REAL subjects and ~34 unique FAKE subjects means the test split consists of only 6 unseen identities per class.


4. CONCRETE RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WORK & PROJECT UPGRADES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To achieve production-grade 85%+ test accuracy on unseen videos, the following steps are recommended:
1. Expand Dataset:
   - Extract 10,000+ balanced face frames across at least 500+ diverse video sources from standard benchmarks (FaceForensics++ / Celeb-DF-v2).
2. Frequency Domain Analysis:
   - Complement spatial MobileNetV2 with frequency-domain features (FFT / Discrete Cosine Transform / SRM filters) to detect spectral artifacts from GAN generators.
3. Multi-Frame Spatio-Temporal Modeling:
   - Use 3D CNNs or LSTM/GRU temporal modules across video frames to detect eye-blink anomalies, head pose inconsistencies, and temporal jitter.
4. Hard Negative Mining:
   - Include webcam images, high-compression videos, and diverse ethnicities during training to ensure high cross-dataset generalization.
